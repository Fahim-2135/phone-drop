Phone Drop

Send photos, files and text between your phone and this PC.

Waiting for your phone…
  1. Scan the code with your phone's camera and open the link.
  2. In Chrome, tap ⋮ → Add to Home screen.
  3. Open Phone Drop from that icon whenever your phone is on the same Wi-Fi as this PC. It finds the PC by itself, even on a different Wi-Fi.
